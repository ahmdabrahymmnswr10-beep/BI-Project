# Prioritized Recommendations

These actions use the Olist dataset and describe follow-up priorities. The order-level delivery and review signals do not prove seller responsibility or causation.

## 1. Review sellers with elevated delivery and rating signals

- **Owner:** Seller Operations and Seller Support
- **Action:** Start with seller `54965bbe3e4f07ae045b90b0b8541f52` (PR). Review its routes, order mix and supporting review evidence before deciding what support to offer.
- **Evidence:** E7 records 22 late deliveries from 73 eligible seller-orders (30.1%) and 28 low ratings from 69 valid reviews (40.6%). The E7 qualifying-seller cohort has a 6.7% late rate and 13.4% low-rating rate.
- **Measure follow-up:** Recalculate late orders / orders with valid actual and estimated dates, and low ratings / valid reviews each month. Keep counts and denominators next to the rates.
- **Limitation:** An order with more than one seller contributes the same delivery and review signal to each seller. These metrics guide investigation; they do not assign cause.

## 2. Confirm seller activity changes across periods

- **Owner:** Seller Management
- **Action:** Contact sellers with sales in January–June 2017 but no recorded sales in January–June 2018; confirm current activity and whether assistance is needed.
- **Evidence:** E6 identifies 421 sellers active only in the 2017 comparison period, 1,473 active only in 2018, and 499 active in both.
- **Measure follow-up:** Track confirmed active sellers and delivered seller-orders in the next reporting period.
- **Limitation:** No observed sale in a six-month window does not prove that a seller closed or churned.

## 3. Monitor coverage and concentration in major customer markets

- **Owner:** Marketplace Planning
- **Action:** Review seller assortment and customer concentration in SP, RJ, MG, RS and PR. Use O1 and O2 to identify sellers whose sales come from a narrow category set or a small group of customers.
- **Evidence:** E4 shows these as the five largest customer states by merchandise sales. O1 reports a 2.16-category average per seller and a 100% median share from the leading category. O2 reports a median 87.9% top-five-customer share.
- **Measure follow-up:** Compare monthly seller sales, active sellers, category breadth and top-five-customer share in those markets.
- **Limitation:** Small sellers naturally have concentrated sales; these comparisons are descriptive and do not establish that broader assortment causes higher sales.
